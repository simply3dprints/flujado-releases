FluJado Golf Sim Hub 0.9.0-beta.12 - 16 October 2026

Changes since beta.6:
- Guided setup with automatic discovery of supported simulators and connectors, executable-location checks and screen selection. Detection is not hardware certification.
- Optional Movies & TV folder with 22 services, local logos and US/Europe filters. Opens in the dashboard with Back navigation; websites use your default browser and your own subscriptions.
- Optional standalone empty tile for your own app.
- Cinema artwork for the Movies & TV folder.
- Redesigned settings with an overview, sidebar navigation and collapsible technical controls. Dark tables, scrollbars and expander controls match the FluJado design.
- Cancelled or stopped startup flows are represented correctly in diagnostic reports.

Existing settings and licensing storage are preserved during upgrades. No simulator software is bundled or reconfigured by this installer.
Unsigned closed beta with test licensing. Physical hardware validation remains specific to tested combinations. Voice control is a future Pro roadmap item, not part of this release.
